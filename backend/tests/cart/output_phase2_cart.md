## Notes

1. **Authentication**: All cart endpoints require authentication (`protect` middleware)
2. **Data Isolation**: Each user has their own cart (user-based filtering)
3. **Snapshot Pricing**: Cart stores price at time of addition (doesn't sync with product price changes)
4. **No Stock Validation**: Cart doesn't validate against product stock (deferred to checkout)
5. **Auto Cleanup**: Invalid items (null products) are automatically removed on getCart
6. **Quantity Merge**: Adding duplicate items (same product+size+color) merges quantity
7. **No Guest Cart**: Current implementation doesn't support guest carts (requires authentication)
